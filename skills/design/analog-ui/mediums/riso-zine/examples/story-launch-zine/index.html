Product launch as a risograph zine

A playful, editorial format to announce a launch with personality.

Use case
Launch a new product
Medium
Risograph zine
Why this works
Feels human, memorable, and turns a product announcement into a story people want to read.

Page thumbnails

    View modes

    Design rationale

    • Fits the momentA launch is a story worth telling; a zine makes it one people pass around.
    • Distinctive and humanTwo inks on paper feel crafted, unlike a typical product page.
    • Easy to scanShort sections and pictures explain the product page by page.

    Interaction details

    • Page navigationArrow buttons, arrow keys, or swipe.
    • Page indicatorShows the current page and the total.
    • ZoomClick or tap a page to read the fine details.
    • ThumbnailsJump straight to any page.
    • ResponsiveSpread on desktop; one page at a time on mobile.

    How it's built

    • Pages3:4 pages separated into pink and blue, then printed with grain and drum drift.
    • ViewerCSS 3D page turns and a little JavaScript. No library.
    • LegibleEvery page zooms to 1800px for the fine print.
    • PerformanceWebP pages in three sizes, loaded only when needed.
    • AccessibilityKeyboard control, each page's words as alt text, visible focus.
