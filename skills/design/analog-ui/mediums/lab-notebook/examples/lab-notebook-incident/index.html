INC-0412: search slowed to 9 seconds

Tue 14 Jul 2026 · Sev 2 · 52 min impact · Northwind Notes (fictional)

Summary

For 52 minutes, searches took up to 9 seconds instead of under 1. Nothing was lost. A config change shrank the search cache, so most searches went to the slow path. Rolling it back fixed it.

Evidence

What happened

Root cause: the config rename had no check that the new key existed, so the cache silently fell back to a tiny default.
