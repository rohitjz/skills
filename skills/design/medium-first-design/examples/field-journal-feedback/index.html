Pressed specimens

Field notes

  1. Recency beats relevance for most daily searches. Boost notes touched in the last 48 hours.
  2. "Inside files" is really two asks: PDFs at work, photos at home. Ship PDF text first.
  3. Offline matters most to frequent travellers, a small group that searches the most.
  4. Trust is the hidden theme. One failed search undoes several good ones.

Northwind Notes is fictional. Quotes and counts are illustrative for this design sample. In real research, keep quotes verbatim and anonymised, and state the sample size.
