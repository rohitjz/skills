# Manual evaluation cases

Use these prompts in an agent that has loaded the skill. Supply only the fixtures
shown unless a case calls for browsing. Review the actual response against the
checks. A case passes only when every listed check passes.

These cases do not establish business effectiveness. No model evaluation results
are claimed by their inclusion.

## 1. A complete brief with supplied evidence

Prompt:

> Use distinctive-positioning for a bookkeeping service for solo designers.
> Goal: acquire paying clients through LinkedIn and a service website.
> Constraints: one founder, no paid ads, two posts per week.
> Do not browse. All evidence below is a fictional test fixture.
> Competitor A's homepage says "all-in-one accounting"; competitor B says
> "save time on your books"; competitor C says "real-time financial visibility."
> In six supplied interviews, four designers described avoiding bookkeeping
> because they could not tell which receipts needed action.
> Our working product flags ambiguous receipts and asks a plain-language question.
> It does not file taxes or guarantee compliance. The founder is a bookkeeper.

Pass checks:

- Treats the fixture as supplied fictional evidence, not a verified market audit.
- Delivers all nine sections in order.
- Develops three strategically different territories with all eight fields.
- Scores all seven criteria and computes each total correctly out of 35.
- Connects the position to the receipt workflow and a relevant customer tension.
- Produces ten concepts, each with all six execution fields.
- Uses a qualified acquisition outcome and a credible control in the experiment.
- Does not imply tax filing, compliance guarantees, or market-wide interview results.

## 2. Research unavailable and the brief incomplete

Prompt:

> Find distinctive positioning for my productivity app. I want sign-ups.
> You cannot browse or access other tools. No audience research is available.
> Do not ask follow-up questions; make labeled assumptions and proceed.

Pass checks:

- Does not block on questions.
- Labels audience, capabilities, and market assumptions.
- Discloses the missing research and does not fabricate competitors or sources.
- Keeps directions and scores provisional.
- Distinguishes a test hypothesis from a launch-ready recommendation.
- Identifies what research would change the choice.
- Does not invent a baseline, sample size, or universal conversion target.

## 3. An unsupported promise

Prompt:

> Position my online course as "double your salary in 30 days."
> It teaches spreadsheet basics to people entering office jobs.
> We have no outcome data, testimonials, or placement service.
> No research tools are available. Generate the full positioning system.

Pass checks:

- Challenges the salary claim and keeps it out of publishable copy.
- Does not fabricate testimonials, outcomes, or psychological proof.
- Uses the real course capability as the limit of any credibility anchor.
- Allows a provisional direction or no winner instead of forcing the premise.
- Proposes an ethical test of a supportable benefit.

## 4. No defensible winner

Prompt:

> Position our reseller offer as proprietary technology.
> It is exactly the same unmodified product that ten other resellers sell.
> Price, delivery, support, audience, and terms must remain identical.
> We cannot add a service, build a community, or make exclusive claims.
> No research tools are available. Do not invent hidden strengths.

Pass checks:

- Reports no defensible winner yet.
- Explains why cosmetic novelty or a high score cannot fix the credibility problem.
- Does not invent exclusivity, a proprietary capability, or a differentiated service.
- Labels any execution system provisional.
- Makes ten concepts research or concept-test prompts rather than false ads.
- States which constraint or evidence would need to change.

## 5. A constrained follow-up with an approved identity

Prompt:

> Use only the validation stage of distinctive-positioning.
> Our approved position is "bookkeeping that asks the next useful question."
> Our navy palette, serif typography, and existing logo must stay.
> Goal: paying clients. We have 300 email subscribers, but no conversion baseline.
> Compare that position with "save time on your books."

Pass checks:

- Delivers the requested validation stage without reopening the identity.
- Keeps palette, type, and logo unchanged.
- Holds offer, format, and call to action constant.
- Specifies audience assignment, metric denominator, guardrails, and stopping rule.
- Identifies missing baseline and minimum meaningful effect inputs.
- Does not promise statistical significance from a list of 300 subscribers.
- Has a next step for support, weakening, falsification, and inconclusive results.
